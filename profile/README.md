## 👋 hckr - Empowering Developers with Powerful Utilities

hckr is a community-owned collection of developer tools and utilities designed to streamline your development workflow. Through our browser extension, hckr helps developers accomplish required tasks with powerful utilities designed to enhance efficiency and productivity.

### 🌟 What is hckr?

hckr is focused on facilitating the development of applications by helping developers accomplish required tasks through utilities specifically designed to enhance efficiency and productivity. We believe in building tools that make developers' lives easier.

### 🌎 📚 Our Ecosystem

- **[hckr Browser Extension](https://github.com/hckr-tools/hckr-tools-browser-extension)** - A community-owned Chrome browser extension for developers
- **[hckr Website & Documentation](https://github.com/hckr-tools/hckr-tools.github.io)** - Our official website and documentation hub
